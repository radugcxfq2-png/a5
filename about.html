About Timique

Curious about watches. Not precious about them.

Timique is a small, independent site for people who enjoy watches and want to understand what theirs can do.

What you will find

Clear explanations of how watch functions work, how to set them without causing damage, and how to keep a handful of watches in good order. Two long guides and three calculators, kept accurate.

How it is made

Each guide is written from ownership and use, checked against manufacturers’ published instructions, and read by a second person before it goes live. Where a rule varies between movements, we say so and point you to the manual.

What you will not find

No watches for sale, no sponsored features and no rankings of brands. We do not value, authenticate or comment on the resale prospects of any watch, and we never suggest buying one for any reason other than wearing it.

Get in touch

If something is wrong or unclear, tell us through the contact page. Timique is based at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.
